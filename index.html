Data source

ECMWF IFS pressure-level and surface forecast fields delivered through the Open-Meteo ECMWF API. The API is queried directly by this browser. Multi-coordinate sampling is used to construct a lightweight 3D scene.
This is a forecast-field visualization, not an official tropical-cyclone warning product. The selected domain is not automatically detected as a cyclone, and no storm centre or intensity category is inferred.

Methodology

Pressure-level temperature, relative humidity, cloud cover, wind speed and direction are sampled at each grid location. Surface precipitation, 10 m wind and mean sea-level pressure are used for surface diagnostics. Height is approximated from standard pressure-level altitude. The 3D layers are visual encodings of sampled forecast values.
